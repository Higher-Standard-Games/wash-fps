## 1. Public by Design

Washiki Washoi has a global demo system and public match data. **Nothing you do on our servers is private:**

- All gameplay, movements, actions, and match events are recorded.
- All in-game text chat is logged and can be seen by other players.
- This applies to **all** matches, including custom and "private" rooms. Everything on our servers is public, so please don't share anything sensitive, personal, or confidential in chat.

## 2. Your Key Is Your Account

We don't ask for your email, real name, phone number, or a password.

- **Your key:** your account is a unique key saved in your browser, or in a file you export yourself.
- **Keep it safe:** if you clear your browser data, change devices, or lose your backup, we have no way to tell that it's you. **We can't recover lost accounts, usernames, or rankings.**

## 3. What We Record, and Why

Even without an email, some of what we keep can still identify you, so here it is plainly:

- **What:** your username and key, your match stats, recorded demos of your matches (including chat), and the IP address our servers see when you connect.
- **Why:** to run matches, show leaderboards and demos, keep play fair, promote the game, and train our bots (see section 5).
- **Demos are a permanent public record.** We don't edit or remove them, and we may archive them.

## 4. Stats and Leaderboards

We record match results (scores, wins and losses, match length, and combat stats) tied to your key and username:

- **Public:** these stats are public and may appear on leaderboards, top lists, match histories, and community tools.
- **Resets:** we may reset, recalculate, or clear leaderboards and match histories when needed (seasons, balance updates, anti-cheat cleanups, or maintenance).
- **Fair play:** exploiting bugs, stat-padding, or tampering with network traffic to change rankings will get your stats wiped and your key banned.

## 5. Demos, Promotion, and AI Training

Because every match is a public demo, you let us record, show, and share your gameplay, match data, username, and chat as part of those demos, for free and worldwide. We use them for:

- **Promotion:** we may make videos from demos and post them on other sites (such as YouTube, Twitch, or X) for promotion, the community, or fun.
- **AI training:** we use recorded demos and match events (movement, aim, and actions) to train AI models, including the bots you play against. That is a big part of why this game exists. Chat is not used for training.
- **Your own videos:** you're welcome to record, stream, or make videos from any demo or gameplay on washoi.net for your own channels.
