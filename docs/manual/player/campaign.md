# Campaign

The campaign is a story of eight operations played against bots. Open it with
**Campaign** in the lobby's menu, under **Quick play**. The key's hint shows how
many operations you have cleared.

## The operations page

The operations run down the page in order, each with its comic card and its
details beside it. A track down the left joins them: cyan through the
operations you have cleared, gold at the one to play next, dashed beyond. The
page opens at your next operation; scroll for the rest. The count at the top
shows how many you have cleared.

| On the track | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cyan ✓ | Cleared. Shows its game mode and the most frags you scored in a win. **Replay** plays it again. |
| Gold number | The one to play next, with its game mode and **Play**. |
| Grey | Classified: clear the operation before it to unlock it. |

| # | Operation | Map | Mode | Opponents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Cartel Contract | `washoi_amphi2` | Amphi | Cartel King, Easy |
| 02 | Street Riot | `washoi_dm1` | Free for all | Four rioters, Easy |
| 03 | Concrete Strike | `washoi_snd1` | Search & Destroy | You and four crew attack five riot cops, Medium |
| 04 | Turf Duel | `washoi_dm2` | Duel | Glitch Queen, Medium |

## Playing an operation

**Play** fades to black and plays the operation's comic card: one
panel at a time fills the screen while the rest of the card is blurred. A panel
moves on by itself after five seconds; click or press any key to move on
sooner, or **Esc** to skip the rest. Meanwhile the game sets up a private room
with the operation's map and bots, and the match loads as soon as the card is
done.

There is no warmup and no ready-up: as soon as you enter, a 10-second
countdown starts ("get ready", then three, two, one), with everyone held at
their spawn until it reaches zero.

In a campaign match you play the robot, and your opponents wear the operation's
model (the Cartel King is the cartel model, the riot cops are police), whatever
your **Player models** settings say. Your crew in Concrete Strike is a mix of
cartel and riotkitty. Teammates are always drawn green, tinted and outlined,
whatever your colour, tint and outline settings.

Concrete Strike is a sabotage run: your side attacks in every round, with no
swap at the half. The first side to win 10 rounds wins the match, by any
margin: 10-0 at the quickest, or 10-9. The match itself is an ordinary match of its
mode; when it ends, **Return to lobby** brings you back to the operations page.

Win the match to clear the operation and unlock the next one. In free for all,
winning means finishing first, ahead of everyone; a tie for first is a draw. A
loss or a draw clears nothing; try again. Like any private match against bots,
a campaign match counts toward your record and rating.

A campaign match is yours alone: nobody can join it midway or watch it.

## Campaign demos

Campaign matches are recorded like any other. Their demos are not in the shared
**Latest** list or the featured picks; you find them under **Your demos**, for
replays and highlight videos.

## Where progress is kept

Progress belongs to your account: the lobby keeps it, so it follows your
account key to any browser. The browser keeps a copy so the page shows your
progress at once.
