# Your First Match

A walkthrough from opening the game to your first frag. If you already know Quake-family shooters, skim the controls and go.

## 1. Get in

1. Open the game. The first time, it asks what to call you: 2–16 letters, numbers, `_` or `-`. You can leave it empty to keep the name you were given.
2. **Keep your account key.** Open **Profile** and use **Copy key** or **Save as file**. It is the only way to get your account back on another computer. See [Accounts](../multiplayer/lobby.md#accounts).
3. On the **Play** stage pick a mode. For learning, **FFA** (free-for-all) is the most forgiving, and **Duel** is the classic 1 v 1.

The welcome guide appears after account setup. Its first page has the terms of
service: tick **I agree to the terms of service** to continue. It then shows
your current controls and movement tips; use **Go to keyboard setup** to adjust bindings. Reopen it
anytime with **?** in the lobby's bottom-right footer.

## 2. Make practice easy: add bots

Enter any room and press **Add bot**. Bots play only in private rooms, so a
public room turns private when the first bot joins; its invite link still brings
your friends in. Add one or two and set the map vote; the room chooses a standard bot preset
near your private rating, with **medium** as the fallback for a new player.
Private bot matches can update your **private** rating and record, separately from
public play. See [Connecting → In the Ready Room](../multiplayer/connecting.md#in-the-ready-room).

Then **ready up**. When everyone is ready the match loads.

## 3. Warmup

You spawn in a **warmup** where nothing scores and you can try things freely. Press **F4** to ready up; the match starts when everyone has. Use this time to learn the map's layout and to try the controls.

## 4. The controls you need

| Key | Does |
|-----|------|
| **W A S D** | Move. You **run by default** |
| **Mouse** | Aim |
| **Space** | Jump; hold it to keep jumping |
| **Ctrl** or **C** | Crouch |
| **Shift** | Walk (slower) |
| **Mouse 1** | Fire |
| **Mouse 2** | Aim down sights |
| **1 – 9** or **mouse wheel** | Switch weapon |
| **R** | Reload |
| **F** | Pick up or swap a weapon |
| **Tab** | Scoreboard |
| **Esc** | Match menu and settings |

Movement and combat bindings can be changed in **Settings → Controls**. Menu,
scoreboard, chat, console and join keys are fixed; see [Settings](./settings.md).

## 5. What to do first

- **You start with only the Blade.** Every other weapon, armor and health pack is on the map. Moving to pick things up is the game. A weapon icon on the left of the screen lights up when you own it; the number keys switch to it.
- **Pick up armor and health when you are hurt.** Armor absorbs about two thirds of every hit. See [Pickups](./pickups-and-inventory.md).
- **Watch the timers.** Items come back on a schedule, and the **Quad Damage** (×4 damage) comes back every two minutes. In a duel, controlling those timers wins matches.
- **Run out of ammo?** Pull the trigger on the empty gun and, if you pull again, the game swaps you to a weapon that can fire. See [Weapons](./weapons.md#empty-guns-click-then-trade).
- **Jumppads and teleporters** are in the maps. Walk into them. You keep your speed through a teleporter. See [Movement](./movement.md#teleporters).
- **Don't stand on a teleporter exit.** Whoever arrives kills you.
- **Falls from far up hurt.** The drop has to be big, over about 5 jump-heights; see [Fall damage](./movement.md#fall-damage).

## 6. When you die

You have a few seconds. The death screen says who fragged you and how, and when you can respawn. Press **fire** when the bar turns green. In a round mode you wait for the next round, and can **spectate** a teammate. See [HUD](./hud.md).

## 7. Getting better

Learn these in roughly this order:

1. **Strafe jumping.** Jump and steer in the air with the side keys and the mouse. See [Movement](./movement.md#strafe-jumping-air-strafing).
2. **Bunny hopping.** Chain jumps with perfect timing to keep your speed.
3. **Rocket jumps.** Shoot the floor under you with the Rocket Launcher to launch yourself. See [Damage and Combat](./combat.md#knockback).
4. **Wall jumps and slides.** See [Movement](./movement.md).
5. **Spectate good players.** Watching someone shows exactly what they see, including their weapon and timers. See [Spectating](./hud.md#spectating), or watch recorded matches on the lobby's **Demos** page.

## 8. When it ends

The result screen shows the winner and your frags. **Back to the lobby** puts you back in the same room for a rematch. See [Match Flow and Screens](./screens.md).
